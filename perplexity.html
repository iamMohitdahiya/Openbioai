Perplexity AI

What is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity AI is an AI-powered search and answer engine that provides accurate, up-to-date information with cited sources. It combines web search with advanced language models to deliver reliable answers.

Key Features

  • AI Search Engine
  • Real-time Web Search
  • Source Citations
  • Research Assistant
  • Document Analysis
  • Fast AI Answers

Pricing

A free version is available. Perplexity Pro offers access to premium AI models, advanced search capabilities and additional features.
