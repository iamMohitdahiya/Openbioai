What is Grok AI?

Grok is an AI assistant developed by xAI. It is designed for conversational AI, reasoning, coding, research and answering real-time questions with a distinctive style.

Key Features

  • Conversational AI
  • Reasoning
  • Coding Assistant
  • Real-time Information
  • Research
  • Creative Writing

Pricing

Grok is available through X subscriptions, with premium plans providing access to advanced AI models and features.
