Gemini AI

What is Gemini AI?

Gemini is Google's multimodal AI assistant. It can understand text, images, audio and code, helping users with writing, research, programming and everyday productivity.

Key Features

  • Multimodal AI
  • Content Writing
  • Coding Assistant
  • Image Understanding
  • Research & Learning
  • Google Workspace Integration

Pricing

Gemini offers a free version, while Gemini Advanced provides access to more powerful models and premium features.
